NEWCHAE · BRITZMEDISafety First
Safety First

Read this before
first use.

NEWCHAE is a personal beauty device, not a medical instrument. Read every caution carefully.

⚠ Do Not Use If

  • Implanted electronics — defibrillators, neurostimulators, or any implanted electronic device.
  • Pregnancy or children — use is not permitted. Consult a specialist.
  • Heart disease, acute medical conditions, or metal allergy.

During Use

  • Always start at level 1. Never exceed your skin's tolerance.
  • Avoid eyes and throat. Lower the level on forehead and around eyes.
  • If tingling or redness persists beyond 24 hours, stop and consult a specialist.

Use With Caution If

  • Skin treatment in progress, allergies, or special skin conditions — consult a specialist.
  • Recent facial surgery — wait until fully recovered.
  • Sensitive skin — perform a patch test on the wrist first.
  • Surgical sites or wounds — consult a specialist before use.
  • Orthodontic appliances — consult your orthodontist.
  • Thyroid conditions — consult a medical specialist.

Never

  • Submerge the device in water or use near bathtubs, showers, or pools.
  • Disassemble the product or use it for medical purposes.
  • Apply more than 2 consecutive shots to one area.
  • Over-stimulate any area or extend sessions beyond 10 minutes.
  • Handle with wet hands or place near heat sources.
Note
This product is not a medical device and should not be used for medical treatment.

Operating & Care

Buttons, battery,
and the LED ring.

Basic Operation

  • Hold power button 1–2 seconds to turn on or off.
  • Short press M to cycle modes — RF → EMS → ELP.
  • Short press power to step through levels — 1 → 2 → 3.
  • Hold M button 1–2 seconds to toggle vibration on/off.

Sessions & Alerts

  • Each session is 10 minutes — auto-off.
  • Usage-time alert beeps every 2 minutes 30 seconds.
  • If charger is connected during use, device switches to charging mode.

Battery & Charging

  • Charge with USB-C cable and adapter rated DC 5V, 1A or higher.
  • Level LED blinks while charging; all three steady when full.
  • If device won't power on, charge first. Disconnect cable before use.

Cleaning & Storage

  • Wipe electrode head with a damp tissue, dry with a soft cloth.
  • Store away from children, direct sunlight, heat, and humidity.
  • Use the included pouch for dust protection.
  • Avoid impact, alcohol, or abrasive cleaners on the polished stainless steel electrode.

LED Reference

Level LEDs blinking → charging. All steady → full. Blinks on power-on but won't start → low battery. Mode LED steady → active mode.

Q1. Can I use the device every day?

Yes — adjust time and frequency to your skin condition. Generally, each mode 2–3 times per week is recommended.

Q2. How do I clean the device?

Wipe the electrode head with a wet tissue, then gently dry with a soft cloth.

Q3. I have a thyroid condition. Can I use it?

Please consult with your physician before using the device.

Q4. I am pregnant. Can I use it?

Pregnant women and children are advised not to use the device. Consult your physician.

Q5. I have orthodontic appliances. Can I use it?

This depends on the type of appliance. Please consult your physician.

Q6. The device won't turn on.

Charge the device. If already charging, disconnect the cable and try again. If the issue persists, contact customer service. Warranty: 1 year from purchase.

Q7. How long per day?

Recommended daily usage is 10 minutes. Adjust based on your skin condition.

Q8. Must I use NEWCHAE Cream?

Other products may work but can reduce RF energy delivery. NEWCHAE Peptide Lifting Cream is formulated to enhance energy delivery and active ingredient penetration.

Q9. Is efficacy the same as clinic devices?

This is a home-care version of our professional multi-channel product. Consistent use with the Cream can help improve skin elasticity and facial contours.

Q10. How is NEWCHAE different from other RF products?

NEWCHAE uses multi-channel technology from professional devices for deeper, more concentrated energy. EMS supports lasting elasticity. ELP enhances ingredient penetration.

NEWCHAE · BRITZMEDIFrequently Asked
Troubleshooting

Special cases
& tips.

Q11. I don't feel vibration.

Hold the M button for 1–2 seconds to toggle vibration. All modes work normally even without vibration.

Q12. I don't hear sound or feel vibration in RF mode.

RF operates automatically — hold the electrode head against skin for 1–2 seconds without moving. The beep signals each shot is complete, then move to the next area.

Q13. The device doesn't turn on while charging.

If the charger is connected, the device switches to charging mode. Disconnect the charger before use — even after full charge.

Q14. I don't feel much stimulation in EMS mode.

NEWCHAE's EMS and ELP are tuned to reduce tingling while staying effective. For a stronger sensation, apply a light watery skincare layer before EMS. Consistent use yields the best results.

Q15. Can I use it on sensitive skin, pigmentation, or filler areas?

For sensitive skin, do a wrist patch test or consult a specialist. Avoid filler-injected areas until fully settled. For pigmentation-prone skin, use a lower setting and follow the recommended duration.
