PROBLEM

The Problem with Conventional RF

Three architectures. Each structurally limited.

MONOPOLAR

Deep, but uncontrollable.

e.g. Thermage

Current travels from a single electrode through the body to a grounding pad. Energy passes through skin, fat, muscle and organs indiscriminately.

REPORTED RISKS

Grounding-pad burns · fat atrophy · significant pain. Complication rate ~10%.

BIPOLAR

Safe, but locked at the surface.

e.g. Venus Viva

Current flows only between two fixed electrodes on the handpiece. No grounding pad — but the electrodes are physically fixed.

STRUCTURAL LIMIT

Penetration depth locked at 1–4 mm. Cannot reach subcutaneous fat. Superficial only.

NEEDLE RF

Deep — at the cost of invasion.

e.g. Morpheus8 · Potenza

Micro-needles penetrate the skin to deliver RF at depth. Effective, but invasive — bleeding, downtime, infection risk.

FDA SAFETY COMMUNICATION · OCT 2025

Burns · scarring · fat loss · facial disfigurement · nerve damage.

PHYSICS

The Physics — D/2 Rule

Why conventional bipolar depth is locked.

In bipolar RF, energy penetration depth equals approximately half the distance between electrodes. This is a well-established physical law.

THE IMPLICATION

To change depth, you must change electrode spacing. In conventional bipolar devices, that spacing is physically fixed — so depth cannot be adjusted. This is not a design flaw; it is a structural limitation of the two-electrode architecture.
