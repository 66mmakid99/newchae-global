Basic Operation

Power &
Controls.

Power ON / OFF

  1. Press and hold the power button for 1–2 seconds to turn ON.
  2. Press the M button briefly to select the operating mode (RF / EMS / ELP).
  3. Press the power button briefly to select the level (1 / 2 / 3).
  4. Press and hold the power button for 1–2 seconds to turn OFF.
Auto-Off
The device automatically turns off after 10 minutes of operation. A notification sound occurs every 2 minutes 30 seconds during use.
Additional Controls

Vibration Setting

Press and hold the M button for 1–2 seconds to toggle vibration on or off.

Battery Charging

  1. Connect the USB-C cable with an appropriate adapter.
  2. Level indicator LEDs blink while charging.
  3. All LEDs are lit when fully charged.
  4. The device will not operate while the charger is connected.

Low Battery

When the battery is low, the LEDs will blink when you try to power on, but the device will not start. Please charge before use.

RF Mode

1 MHz · Automatic Shot System

Delivers multi-channel high-frequency energy deep into the skin to help with volume management. Recommended 2–3 times per week, up to 10 minutes per session.

  1. Cleanse your face thoroughly with lukewarm water.
  2. Apply NEWCHAE Peptide Lifting Cream generously, ensuring the electrodes are fully covered.
  3. Turn on the device and select RF mode & level.
  4. Hold electrode head against skin — a beep signals the shot is complete, move to the next area.
  5. Move the electrode head from inside to outside of the face.
  6. The device automatically turns off after 10 minutes.
Do not apply more than two consecutive shots to the same area. Always start at Level 1. Lower the level for forehead and around the eyes.

NEWCHAE · BRITZMEDIPrecautions
Precautions

Before
You Begin.

  • Read the user manual thoroughly before use.
  • Consult a specialist if you are undergoing skin treatment or have allergies.
  • Do not use if you have a metal allergy.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
  • Do not use with heart disease, acute conditions, or implanted devices (pacemaker, neurostimulator).
  • For sensitive skin, perform a patch test on the wrist first.
  • After facial surgery, use only after full recovery.
  • Mild stinging, warmth, or redness may occur within 24 hours and typically resolves in 2–3 days.
  • Avoid using around the eyes or on the throat area.
  • Always start at Level 1. Do not apply more than two shots to one area.
  • Do not handle with wet hands or expose to strong impacts.
  • This product is not a medical device and should not be used for medical treatment.

⚠ Warning

  • Do not immerse the main unit in water.
  • Ensure no liquids enter the main unit.
  • Do not use near bathtubs, showers, or pools.
  • Do not disassemble the product.
  • Do not place near heat sources — may cause fire.
  • If unusual noise, smell, or smoke occurs, stop use and contact customer service.
  • Do not use a product that has been submerged in water.

Storage Instructions

  • Wipe with a wet tissue, then dry gently with a soft cloth.
  • Store away from direct sunlight in a cool, dry place.
  • Prevent damage from strong pressure or impacts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q&A.

Q1. Can I use the device every day?

Yes. We recommend adjusting time and frequency to your skin condition. Generally, use each mode 2–3 times per week.

Q2. How do I clean the device?

Wipe the electrode head with a wet tissue, then dry gently with a soft cloth or towel.

Q3. I am pregnant. Can I use it?

Pregnant women and children are advised not to use the device. Consult your physician before use.

Q4. How long should I use per day?

Recommended daily usage is 10 minutes. Adjust based on your skin condition.

Q5. Must I use NEWCHAE Cream?

Other products may work, but may reduce RF energy delivery. NEWCHAE Cream is formulated to enhance energy delivery and active ingredient penetration.

Q6. How is NEWCHAE different from other RF devices?

NEWCHAE uses multi-channel technology from professional products for deeper, more concentrated energy. EMS medium-frequency maintains elasticity longer. ELP enhances active ingredient penetration.

Q7. The device won't turn on.

Charge the device, or disconnect the cable if already charging. If the issue persists, contact customer service. Warranty: 1 year from purchase.

Q8. I don't feel vibration.

Press and hold the M button for 1–2 seconds to toggle vibration. All modes work normally even without vibration.
