How to Use — 3 Modes
RF

RF Mode

1 MHz · Auto Shot

High-frequency energy for deep skin care and volume management.

  1. Cleanse face with lukewarm water.
  2. Apply NEWCHAE cream over treatment area.
  3. Select RF mode & level.
  4. Hold on skin — beep signals to move.
  5. Move inside → outside.
  6. Auto-off after 10 min.
Max 2 shots per area. Start Level 1.
EMS

EMS Mode

1 KHz · Medium Freq.

Electrical stimulation for skin elasticity and contour management.

  1. Cleanse face with lukewarm water.
  2. Apply NEWCHAE cream generously.
  3. Select EMS mode & level.
  4. Glide upward: bottom → top, inside → outside.
  5. Auto-off after 10 min.
Start Level 1. Avoid over-stimulation.
ELP

ELP Mode

5–20 KHz · Multi Freq.

Electroporation for enhanced absorption of active ingredients.

  1. Cleanse face with lukewarm water.
  2. Apply NEWCHAE cream generously.
  3. Select ELP mode & level.
  4. Massage in small circles, inside → outside.
  5. Auto-off after 10 min.
Start Level 1. Avoid over-stimulation.
Recommended Cycle Use each mode 2–3 times per week, up to 10 minutes per session.

Precautions
Before You Begin.
  • Read the user manual thoroughly before use.
  • Consult a specialist if undergoing skin treatment or have allergies.
  • Do not use if you have a metal allergy.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
  • Do not use with heart disease, acute conditions, or implanted devices.
  • For sensitive skin, patch test on wrist first.
  • After facial surgery, use only after full recovery.
  • Mild stinging or redness may occur, typically resolves in 2–3 days.
  • Avoid using around the eyes or on the throat area.
  • Always start at Level 1. Max 2 shots per area.
  • Do not handle with wet hands or expose to impacts.
  • This is not a medical device. Not for medical treatment.

Warning

  • Do not immerse the device in water.
  • Ensure no liquids enter the unit.
  • Do not use near bathtubs, showers, or pools.
  • Do not disassemble the product.
  • Keep away from heat sources — fire risk.
  • Stop use if unusual noise, smell, or smoke occurs.

Storage

  • Wipe with wet tissue, dry with soft cloth.
  • Store in cool, dry place away from sunlight.
  • Avoid strong pressure or impacts.

FAQ

Q. Can I use it every day?

Yes. Recommended 2–3 times per week per mode, up to 10 min.

Q. Must I use NEWCHAE Cream?

Others may work but can reduce RF delivery. NEWCHAE Cream is formulated for optimal energy transfer.

Q. I am pregnant. Can I use it?

Not recommended. Consult your physician.

Q. The device won't turn on.

Charge the device. Disconnect cable if charging. Warranty: 1 year.
