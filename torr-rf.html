What is TORR RF

Toroidal Multipolar RF. Made in Korea.

TORR RF is a professional aesthetic medical device that delivers multi-wave radiofrequency energy through a patented toroidal electrode array. Unlike conventional monopolar or bipolar systems, the toroidal architecture creates a 3D electromagnetic field — enabling uniform energy distribution across multiple tissue depths simultaneously.

The Smart Combination system integrates monopolar-grade deep penetration (5–15mm) and bipolar-grade surface precision (2.5–5mm) into a single handpiece. Clinicians switch between Deep Mode and Low Mode to target everything from subdermal fat to superficial fine lines — without changing handpieces, without cooling systems, without grounding pads.

Designed and manufactured by BRITZMEDI in Seongnam, Korea. US FDA 510(k) cleared (K212561), MFDS Class III licensed (No. 22-156), ISO 13485:2016 certified, GMP compliant. Zero consumables — no cartridges, no tips, no recurring costs.

Core Technology

Smart Combination.
Two modes, one handpiece.

The toroidal multipolar electrode array enables dual-mode operation — combining monopolar-grade deep penetration with bipolar-grade surface precision. No cooling system. No grounding pad.

D

Deep Mode — Monopolar Path

Target: 5–15mm+ (subdermal to fat)

Long-path current flows between distant ring electrodes. Deep tightening, lifting, localized fat reduction, submental contouring.

L

Low Mode — Bipolar Path

Target: 2.5–5mm (superficial to mid-dermis)

Short-path current between adjacent ring electrodes. Fine wrinkles, pore refinement, texture improvement, collagen remodeling.
