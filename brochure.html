Core Technology

Built on patents,
not hype.

Two registered RF patents. Nearly a decade of in-house R&D behind every shot.

NEWCHAE Multi-Shot RF

The advantages

  • Multi-channel delivery — energy distributed across multiple channels simultaneously.
  • Uniform dermal heat — even penetration reaches the target layer without hot spots.
  • Lower power, higher efficacy — safer surface, deeper effect.
  • Patent-protected design — 2 KIPO registered RF patents held by BRITZMEDI.
Conventional RF

The limitations

  • Single-channel delivery concentrates energy at one point.
  • Uneven heat distribution can cause surface hot spots.
  • Higher power required to reach target depth.
  • Greater risk of discomfort on sensitive skin.
