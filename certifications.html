🇰🇷Korea KC EMC (Device) KR24-HEK0239 Eurofins KCTL Korea Radio Act Art. 8 — KS C 9814-1, KS C 9814-2 (EMC for household appliances) 2024-04-05 PDF ▾
What is this?

Proves NEWCHAE doesn't interfere with other electronics (Wi-Fi, TV, etc.) and isn't affected by them either. It passed Korea's official electromagnetic compatibility test for household appliances.

Scope

The device body itself — the main unit that emits RF, EMS, and ELP energy. Separate from the battery certification below.

Why it matters

Required for legal sale in South Korea. Cannot list on Coupang, Naver, or any Korean retail without it. Also serves as baseline evidence for CE and FCC applications.
🇰🇷Korea KC Safety (Battery) XH100102-18019D KTC KC 62133:2019-02 — Li-ion battery safety (Applicant: EJENs Co., Ltd.) 2020-06-26 PDF ▾
What is this?

The built-in lithium battery (Samsung SDI cells, assembled by EJENs) won't overheat, catch fire, or explode. Passed overcharge, short circuit, drop, high temperature, and crush tests.

Scope

The rechargeable battery pack only (LI-35E 101, 3.6V/3,350mAh). Korean law requires separate safety certification for the device body and its battery.

Why it matters

Korean marketplaces require both KC EMC + KC Battery to list any rechargeable beauty device. This pair is the entry ticket to the Korean market.
🇪🇺EU CE (EMC) KR24-SEC0221 Eurofins KCTL EMC Directive 2014/30/EU — EN IEC 55014-1:2021, EN IEC 55014-2:2021, EN/IEC 61000-3-2, EN/IEC 61000-3-3 2024-10-18 PDF ▾
What is this?

The CE mark is Europe's mandatory safety passport. This cert covers EMC — proving the device plays nicely with other electronics. Covers both the NWP and NC models.

Scope

All 27 EU member states + EEA (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein). UK also accepts CE. Recognized as baseline in Turkey, Israel, and parts of Southeast Asia.

Why it matters

Without CE marking, EU customs will reject shipments. The single most important cert for European market access. Required by Amazon EU and all European distributors.
🇪🇺EU Battery Safety KR24-YBC0009 Eurofins KCTL IEC 62133-2:2017+AMD1:2021, EN 62133-2:2017+A1:2021 (Battery: EJENs LI-35E 101) 2024-08-29 PDF ▾
What is this?

EU equivalent of Korea's battery safety cert. The battery passed European safety standards for overheating, short-circuit, crushing, and abnormal charging.

Scope

Battery pack for EU market entry. Works alongside CE EMC — together they cover electromagnetic safety and battery safety, the two main requirements.

Why it matters

Required to complete the CE Declaration of Conformity. Amazon EU and many European retailers explicitly request this certificate before listing.
🇺🇸US FCC SDoC KR24-YEF0068 BRITZMEDI (Self-declaration) FCC Rules Part 15 — Supplier's Declaration of Conformity 2024 PDF ▾
What is this?

The FCC requires all US-sold electronics don't cause harmful radio interference. NEWCHAE has no wireless, so it qualifies as "unintentional radiator" — SDoC is sufficient, no full FCC ID needed.

Scope

Entire United States. Covers unintentional radio emissions. Note: a US-based "Responsible Party" address still needs to be designated for full compliance.

Why it matters

Amazon US, Walmart, and other retailers require FCC compliance docs to list electronic devices. Also useful for Canada (ISED) preparation.
🌎EU / Global RoHS (Housing) F690101/LF-CTSAYAA24-01205 SGS Korea IEC 62321 series — RoHS 10 substances (PMMA housing material; supplier: LX MMA Corp.) All N.D. 2024-01-15 PDF ▾
What is this?

RoHS = Restriction of Hazardous Substances. The outer casing (PMMA acrylic) is free of toxic heavy metals, flame retardants, and phthalates — all 10 restricted substances: "Not Detected."

Scope

External housing material only (PMMA by LX MMA Corp.), not the entire device. A whole-product RoHS DoC covering all components would strengthen EU compliance.

Why it matters

EU and many Asian markets ban harmful substances above certain thresholds. Tested by SGS, a globally recognized lab — carries weight with distributors and customs.
