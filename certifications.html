Korea · KC

KC EMC (Device Body)

Electromagnetic Compatibility Test Certificate
Standard
KS C 9814-1, KS C 9814-2
Issued by
Eurofins KCTL
Date
April 5, 2024
Model
BD-ERD24NWP
PDF KR24-HEK0239
📡

What is this?

This certificate proves that the NEWCHAE device doesn't interfere with other electronics and isn't affected by them either. It passed Korea's official EMC (electromagnetic compatibility) tests.

What does it cover?

The device body itself — the main unit that emits RF, EMS, and ELP energy. It confirms the device won't disrupt your Wi-Fi, TV, or other gadgets when in use.

Why does it matter?

Required for legal sale in South Korea. Without this, the device cannot be listed on Coupang, Naver Shopping, or any Korean retail channel. Also serves as a foundation for CE and FCC applications.

Korea · KC

KC Safety (Battery)

Lithium-Ion Battery Safety Certification
Standard
KC 62133:2019-02
Issued by
KTC (Korea Testing Certification)
Date
June 26, 2020
Battery
LI-35E 101 (3.6V / 3,350mAh)
PDF XH100102-18019D
🔋

What is this?

This confirms the built-in lithium battery won't overheat, catch fire, or explode under normal use and stress conditions. The battery (made by EJENs using Samsung SDI cells) passed Korea's official safety tests.

What does it cover?

The rechargeable battery pack only — not the device body. It was tested for overcharging, short circuit, drop impact, high temperature, and crush resistance.

Why does it matter?

Korean law requires separate battery safety certification for any rechargeable device sold domestically. Both this KC battery cert and the KC EMC cert above must be submitted when listing on Korean marketplaces.

EU · CE

CE EMC Attestation

EU Electromagnetic Compatibility Directive Conformity
Directive
EMC Directive 2014/30/EU
Standards
EN IEC 55014-1/2:2021, EN 61000-3-2/3-3
Date
October 18, 2024
Models
BD-ERD24NWP, BD-ERD24NC
PDF KR24-SEC0221
🇬🇧

What is this?

The CE mark is Europe's mandatory safety passport. This specific cert covers the EMC aspect — proving the device plays nicely with other electronic equipment throughout the EU.

What does it cover?

All 27 EU member states + EEA countries (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein). It also makes UK market entry easier since the UK still recognizes CE marking. Covers both the original NEWCHAE model and the NC variant.

Why does it matter?

Without CE marking, you cannot legally sell or import the device anywhere in Europe. Customs will reject shipments. This cert is also accepted as a baseline in many non-EU countries (Turkey, Israel, parts of Southeast Asia).

EU · Battery Safety

EN 62133-2 Test Report

EU Lithium-Ion Battery Safety Test
Standard
IEC/EN 62133-2:2017+A1:2021
Issued by
Eurofins KCTL
Date
August 29, 2024
Battery
EJENs LI-35E 101 (Samsung SDI cell)
PDF KR24-YBC0009
⚡

What is this?

The EU equivalent of Korea's battery safety cert. The battery was tested under European safety standards for overheating, short-circuiting, crushing, and abnormal charging — and passed all tests.

What does it cover?

The battery pack for EU market entry. This works alongside the CE EMC cert above. Together, they cover the two main safety concerns for a rechargeable beauty device: electromagnetic safety and battery safety.

Why does it matter?

EU regulations require battery safety evidence to complete the CE Declaration of Conformity. Many EU retailers and Amazon EU explicitly request this certificate. Also recognized by the UK.

USA · FCC

FCC SDoC

Supplier's Declaration of Conformity (Part 15)
Regulation
FCC Rules Part 15
Declaration by
BRITZMEDI Co., Ltd.
Date
2024
Product
Skin Care Device · NEWCHAE Shot
PDF KR24-YEF0068
📡

What is this?

The FCC (Federal Communications Commission) requires that all electronic devices sold in the US don't cause harmful radio interference. This self-declaration, backed by lab testing, confirms NEWCHAE complies.

What does it cover?

The entire United States. Covers unintentional radio emissions from the device. Since NEWCHAE doesn't use wireless communication (no Bluetooth/Wi-Fi), it falls under Part 15 "unintentional radiator" rules, which require SDoC rather than full FCC ID registration.

Why does it matter?

Amazon US, Walmart, and other US retailers require FCC compliance documentation before listing electronics. Note: a US-based "Responsible Party" address needs to be added to the declaration for full compliance.

Global · RoHS

RoHS Test Report

Restriction of Hazardous Substances — PMMA Housing Material
Standard
IEC 62321 series
Issued by
SGS Korea
Date
January 15, 2024
Material
PMMA (LX MMA Corp.)
PDF F690101 / LF-CTSAYAA24-01205
🌿

What is this?

RoHS stands for Restriction of Hazardous Substances. This test confirms the device's outer casing material (PMMA plastic) is free of toxic heavy metals, flame retardants, and phthalates — all 10 restricted substances came back "Not Detected."

What does it cover?

The external housing material only (PMMA acrylic supplied by LX MMA Corp.), not the entire finished device. For full EU RoHS compliance, a whole-product Declaration of Conformity (DoC) covering all components would strengthen the package.

Why does it matter?

The EU and many Asian markets ban products containing harmful substances above certain thresholds. This cert shows the most consumer-facing part — the casing you hold — is clean. Tested by SGS, one of the world's most recognized testing labs.
